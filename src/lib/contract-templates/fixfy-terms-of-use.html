(a) Platform Bookings whose money became due for payout by the end of that Sunday: you completed the Booking, submitted the completion report, we checked it, the Customer has paid in full and no complaint is open; and

(b) Fixfy Client Work you completed and we approved by the end of that Sunday.

Anything still waiting moves to the payout for the period in which it becomes due.

(b) Fixfy's VAT invoice for its commission and any Late-Withdrawal Fee; and

9.5 Guarantee and remedial visits.

(a) Platform Bookings. Every Platform Booking carries your guarantee to the Customer: 7 days as standard, and for longer-lasting work painting and decorating 3 months, tiling 3 months, wall treatments 6 months, and woodwork or structural work 6 months. (b) Fixfy Client Work. If the Client reports within 7 days of the Booking that something in the Job Details or checklist was not done, or not done to standard, you return to put it right at no extra charge, at a time we agree with the Client, normally within 48 hours of us telling you.
