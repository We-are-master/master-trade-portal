Background

Since April 2026 Fixfy has accounted for VAT on its commission only, on the advice of its accountants. This version, effective 6 October 2026, sets out that arrangement in writing.

How Fixfy works

Fixfy runs a marketplace for home and property services. Customers book Services through Fixfy and independent Partners carry them out. There are two kinds of work, and every Job Offer tells you which one it is.

Platform Bookings (Schedule A). A Customer books through Fixfy's website, WhatsApp, telephone or a Fixfy quote. Your contract for the work is with the Customer. Fixfy is your agent: it markets your Services, quotes the Published Price, arranges the Booking and collects the Customer's payment for you. Fixfy keeps a commission, which is a fixed amount per Service set out in the Commission Schedule (or 30% of the price for quoted work that is not in the price list), and pays you the rest. Customer cancellation charges and call-outs are yours in full.

Fixfy Client Work (Schedule B). Fixfy has its own contract with a business Client, such as a letting agent, a property manager or a service platform. Fixfy engages you as its subcontractor and pays you a fixed Partner Fee for each Job by self-billing.

Fixfy does not charge you to join or use the Platform. The only charges Fixfy makes to you are its commission on Platform Bookings and a £50 Late-Withdrawal Fee if you cancel a Booking with 24 hours' notice or less, or do not attend.

A1. The Contract Is Between You and the Customer

A1.1 For each Platform Booking, the contract for the Service is between you and the Customer. You supply the Service to the Customer. Fixfy is not a party to that contract and does not supply the Service.

A1.2 That contract is on the Customer Terms and the Job Details. You agree that the Customer Terms apply to every Platform Booking, and you authorise Fixfy to agree them with Customers on your behalf.

A1.3 There are two separate supplies in each Platform Booking:

(a) your supply of the Service to the Customer, for the Published Price; and

(b) Fixfy's supply of agency services to you, for the Commission. Fixfy's agency services are standard-rated for VAT.

A2. Fixfy's Appointment as Your Agent

A2.1 You appoint Fixfy as your agent for each Platform Booking, to:

(a) market your Services to Customers through the Fixfy Channels;

(b) publish and quote Published Prices for your Services;

(c) take bookings and conclude them with Customers on your behalf, in line with clause 4 of the Agreement;

(d) confirm each Booking to the Customer, naming you;

(e) give Customers, on your behalf, the information the law requires before and after they book;

(f) handle cancellations and changes by Customers in line with the Customer Terms;

(g) collect payment from Customers as your limited payment collection agent, and issue receipts and invoices to Customers in your name, under the Invoicing and Payment Collection Agreement;

(h) handle first contact with the Customer on complaints, and arrange guarantee visits; and

(i) make refunds to Customers as this Schedule allows.

A2.2 When Fixfy does these things, it acts in your name and on your behalf. Fixfy does not buy the Service from you and does not resell it to the Customer. Fixfy does not receive or use the Service itself, and it does not change the nature or the value of your supply to the Customer.

A2.3 Fixfy acts only for you in each Platform Booking. It does not act as the Customer's agent.

A2.4 Fixfy's authority is limited to this Schedule. Fixfy cannot commit you to work outside the Job Details or the Customer Terms without your agreement.

A2.5 Fixfy acts as agent for many partners at the same time, including partners who offer the same Services in the same areas. You agree that Fixfy decides which partner to offer each Job to, and that this is not a breach of any duty Fixfy owes you as your agent.

A2.6 A Customer may pay when booking, before any partner has accepted the Booking. Fixfy receives that payment as agent for the partner who accepts the Booking. When you accept, the payment becomes Funds Held for you. If no partner accepts, Fixfy refunds the Customer.

A3. Your Identity and Contact with the Customer

A3.1 Fixfy names you to the Customer in the Booking confirmation and on the receipt or invoice, using the legal name and trading name recorded on your Fixfy account. Invoicing for a Platform Booking is between you and the Customer: Fixfy issues the receipt or invoice in your name and on your behalf, under the Invoicing and Payment Collection Agreement.

A3.2 Fixfy may give the Customer the other information about you that consumer law requires, including your business address. Keep the details on your Fixfy account accurate.

A3.3 On site you may give the Customer your name and trading name.

A3.4 During the Booking, including any guarantee visit, contact with the Customer about the Booking runs through the Fixfy Channels: the Fixfy Trade app, Fixfy's WhatsApp and the Fixfy office. This gives the Customer one record and lets Fixfy help if anything goes wrong. Changes, extra work and payment for the Booking are arranged through Fixfy, not directly with the Customer.

A4. Prices, Quotes and Extra Work

A4.1 Fixfy sets the Published Price for each Service on your behalf, from its price list or from a quote it prepares for the Customer. The Published Price includes any VAT you must charge.

A4.2 By accepting a Platform Booking you agree to carry it out for the Customer at the Published Price shown in the Job Details.

A4.3 If extra work or extra time is needed, tell Fixfy through the Fixfy Trade app before you start. Fixfy quotes it to the Customer on your behalf, at the Published Price for that work or at a price you agree with Fixfy, and collects the payment. Do the extra work only once Fixfy confirms that the Customer has agreed. The Commission on extra work is the amount in the Commission Schedule where the work is in the price list or, for quoted work that is not, 30% of the quoted price including VAT, unless a different amount is agreed with you in writing before you accept it.

A4.4 Price changes, discounts, refunds and compensation for a Platform Booking are agreed through Fixfy, not directly between you and the Customer.

A5. Materials

A5.1 You supply the materials included in the Published Price, including cleaning products for cleaning Services and the paint materials pack for painting Services where the Booking includes it. You buy them yourself, and they are part of your supply to the Customer.

A5.2 Fixfy does not sell materials and does not add any markup to materials.

A5.3 If a Job needs materials that are not included in the Published Price, tell Fixfy before you buy them. Fixfy quotes them to the Customer on your behalf at the price you give it, with no Fixfy markup, and collects the payment for you. Fixfy's Commission on extra materials quoted under this paragraph is 30% of their price, including VAT, as set out in the Commission Schedule.

A6. Payment Collection and Funds Held

A6.1 You appoint Fixfy as your limited payment collection agent for Platform Bookings, on the terms of the Invoicing and Payment Collection Agreement.

A6.2 When a Customer pays Fixfy for a Platform Booking, that payment discharges the Customer's debt to you for the amount paid, as if the Customer had paid you directly. The Customer has no further liability to you for that amount.

A6.3 Fixfy holds Customer payments for Platform Bookings on your behalf as Funds Held until payout. Fixfy keeps Funds Held readily distinguishable in its records from its own taxable supplies, which are its Commission, its fees and its Fixfy Client Work.

A6.4 Funds Held for a Booking become due for payout when:

(a) you have completed the Booking and submitted the completion report;

(b) Fixfy has checked the completion report;

(c) Fixfy has received the Customer's payment in full; and

(d) there is no open complaint about the Booking.

If a complaint is open, Fixfy may hold back the amount in dispute until the complaint is resolved, and pays the rest.

A6.5 Fixfy pays you the Funds Held that are due, less the Commission and any amounts deducted under clause 9.5 of the Agreement, on the next payout date.

A6.6 Fixfy takes reasonable steps to collect what a Customer owes for a Booking, but it does not guarantee payment of an amount the Customer does not pay.

A6.7 Fixfy bears its own payment processing costs. They are never deducted from your net amount.

A6.8 Fixfy keeps Funds Held in a separate account (a dedicated pot of its business bank account) used only for money collected for Partners on Platform Bookings, and records them separately from Fixfy's own money. Funds Held do not earn interest for you.

A7. Commission

A7.1 Fixfy's income from a Platform Booking is the Commission. Fixfy takes no other margin, markup or fee from the Published Price.

A7.2 For a Service in the price list, the Commission is the fixed amount set out in the Commission Schedule. For quoted work that is not in the price list, it is 30% of the quoted price, including VAT, unless a different amount is agreed with you in writing before you accept the Booking. The Commission is inclusive of VAT at the standard rate. Each Job Offer shows it, and it does not change after you accept the Booking.

A7.3 Fixfy deducts the Commission from the Funds Held at payout and issues you a VAT invoice for it with every payout, even though the Commission is deducted rather than paid by you separately.

A8. Fixfy Promotions

A8.1 Fixfy may offer Customers discounts, promotional codes or other promotions. Fixfy funds them.

A8.2 Where a Customer uses a Fixfy promotion, Fixfy pays the promotion amount to you as part of the Published Price, on the Customer's behalf. Your price stays the Published Price and your net amount does not change.

A8.3 The receipt shows the Published Price, the promotion amount paid by Fixfy on the Customer's behalf and the amount the Customer paid.

A9. Cancellations

A9.1 Fixfy handles cancellations and changes by Customers on your behalf, in line with the Customer Terms.

A9.2 Where the Customer Terms charge the Customer a late cancellation charge or a call-out (for example because the Customer cancels late, nobody lets you in or the property is not ready), that charge belongs to you in full. Fixfy collects it on your behalf as Funds Held and pays all of it to you. Fixfy takes no Commission on it.

A9.3 To claim a call-out because you could not get in or could not carry out the Booking, wait at least 30 minutes from the start of the arrival window and tell Fixfy through the Fixfy Trade app while you are at the property, with a photo.

A9.4 If the Customer cancels free of charge under the Customer Terms, including under any legal right to cancel, Fixfy refunds the Customer from the Funds Held and nothing is payable to you for that Booking.

A9.5 If you cancel a Platform Booking, or do not attend it, you authorise Fixfy to arrange for another partner to carry it out. Your contract with the Customer for that Booking then ends, and you have no right to the Funds Held for it. The Late-Withdrawal Fee applies if you cancel with 24 hours' notice or less before the arrival window starts, or do not attend.

A10. Guarantee

A10.1 If the Customer reports, within 7 days of completion (or, for longer-lasting work, within your longer guarantee: painting and decorating 3 months, tiling 3 months, wall treatments 6 months, and woodwork or structural work 6 months), that something in the Job Details was not done or was not done to standard, you will return to put it right at no extra cost. Fixfy arranges the return visit with the Customer, and you will attend within 7 days of Fixfy telling you, at the time Fixfy agrees with the Customer.

A10.2 If you cannot or do not put it right, Fixfy may arrange for another partner to do so and recover the reasonable cost from your Funds Held or later payouts. If that is not possible, Fixfy may refund the Customer, on your behalf, for the part of the work that was not right, from your Funds Held or later payouts. Fixfy will tell you the amount and the reason first.

A11. Complaints, Refunds and Chargebacks

A11.1 Fixfy handles first contact with the Customer on any complaint and mediates between you and the Customer. You must answer Fixfy's questions within the time set out in the Terms of Use.

A11.2 Fixfy decides fairly, on the evidence, including the Job Details, your reports, photos and messages, whether you are at fault. Fixfy tells you the outcome and the reasons.

A11.3 Where you are at fault, you authorise Fixfy to refund the Customer, in full or in part, from the Funds Held for the Booking or, if they have been paid out, by deduction from later payouts. Where a guarantee visit is a fair remedy, Fixfy will offer it first.

A11.4 Where you are not at fault, any refund or goodwill gesture Fixfy decides to make is funded by Fixfy and does not reduce your net amount.

A11.5 If a Customer's card payment is reversed by the card issuer and the reason is your work, your conduct or your non-attendance, the reversed amount comes from your Funds Held or later payouts. In any other case Fixfy bears it. Fixfy will challenge a reversal where it has reasonable evidence to do so, and your reports and photos are that evidence.

A11.6 If you disagree with a decision under this paragraph, tell Fixfy within 14 days and Fixfy will review it with you in good faith. Nothing in this paragraph affects the Customer's legal rights against you.

A12. Responsibility for the Work

A12.1 You are responsible to the Customer for the Service, including carrying it out with reasonable care and skill and in line with the law, including the Consumer Rights Act 2015 where the Customer is a consumer.

A12.2 You are responsible for any damage you or Partner Personnel cause, and you hold the insurance set out in clause 13.3 of the Agreement, including public liability insurance of at least £1,000,000 and employers' liability insurance if you employ anyone.

A12.3 Fixfy is responsible to you and to the Customer only for its own agency services.

A13. VAT and Tax

A13.1 The Published Price is the price of your supply to the Customer. The full Published Price, including any promotion paid by Fixfy on the Customer's behalf, counts towards your turnover for VAT and for income tax or corporation tax. You are responsible for monitoring the VAT registration threshold.

A13.2 If you are VAT registered, you account to HMRC for VAT on the full Published Price, and each receipt or invoice Fixfy issues in your name shows your VAT number. The Published Price includes VAT and does not increase because you are registered.

A13.3 If you are not VAT registered, receipts issued in your name show no VAT. You must sign the VAT Status Declaration in the Invoicing and Payment Collection Agreement and tell Fixfy within 14 days of any change to your VAT status.

A13.4 Fixfy accounts for VAT only on its Commission and its fees.

A13.5 Fixfy makes no Construction Industry Scheme deductions from Platform Bookings, because Fixfy is not the contractor. It passes on the Customer's payment as your agent.

Schedule B: Fixfy Client Work

This Schedule applies to every Booking of Fixfy Client Work. Words defined in the Agreement have the same meaning here.

B1. Fixfy Is the Principal

B1.1 For Fixfy Client Work, Fixfy has its own contract with the Client and is responsible to the Client for the work. Fixfy engages you as its subcontractor to carry out the Job.

B1.2 Each Booking of Fixfy Client Work forms a separate contract between you and Fixfy on the terms of the Agreement, the Terms of Use and the Job Details. You do not have a contract with the Client, but you must follow the Client's requirements and site rules set out in the Job Details.

B1.3 The relationship with the Client belongs to Fixfy. You must not agree prices, extra work, refunds or compensation with a Client or resident, or discuss your Partner Fee with them. Pass any such request to Fixfy through the Platform.

B2. Partner Fee

B2.1 For each Booking of Fixfy Client Work, Fixfy pays you the Partner Fee shown in the Job Details when you accepted it. It is a fixed amount for the Job, or a fixed rate per hour, half day or day paid for the time Fixfy approves. The price the Client pays Fixfy is a matter between Fixfy and the Client.

B2.2 A Booking becomes payable once you have completed it, submitted the completion report the Platform asks for, and Fixfy has approved it.

B2.3 All Partner Fees are inclusive of any VAT. If you are VAT registered, each self-bill shows the VAT included.

B2.4 Fixfy may change the Partner Fees it offers for future Jobs at any time. A change never affects a Booking you have already accepted.

B3. Materials

B3.1 Fixed-price Jobs include the consumables you normally need for that kind of work. If a Job needs materials that are not included, check with Fixfy through the Platform before you buy them. Fixfy reimburses approved materials at cost when you record them in the completion report with a photo of the receipt.

B4. How You Attend

B4.1 For Fixfy Client Work you attend on Fixfy's behalf, as its independent subcontractor. Follow the Client's rules on identification, conduct and communication set out in the Job Details.

B4.2 Do not hand out your own business cards, prices or contact details to the Client or residents, and do not mention any other company or platform to them, except as the Job Details say.

B5. Cancellations and Changes by Fixfy or the Client

B5.1 Fixfy may change, reallocate or cancel a Booking of Fixfy Client Work in the cases in clause 4.6 of the Agreement, and will give you as much notice as it can. Apart from paragraph B5.2, Fixfy is not liable to you for lost fees, Partner Personnel costs or travel costs.

B5.2 Fixfy pays you a call-out of £25, unless the Job Details say a different amount, if:

(a) the Client cancels the Booking with 24 hours' notice or less; or

(b) you cannot carry out the Booking because of the Client, for example because nobody lets you in or the property is not ready, and you tell Fixfy through the Fixfy Trade app while you are at the property, with a photo.

B5.3 You agree the call-out is a genuine pre-estimate of your loss, and you will not claim anything further from Fixfy or the Client for that cancellation.

B6. Remedial Work

B6.1 If the Client reports within 7 days of the Booking that something in the Job Details or checklist was not done, or not done to standard, you will return to put it right at no extra charge, at a time Fixfy agrees with the Client, normally within 48 hours of Fixfy telling you.

B6.2 If you cannot or do not, Fixfy may arrange for someone else to do it and deduct the reasonable cost from your payouts.

B7. Complaints

B7.1 Fixfy decides how to resolve any complaint or dispute with the Client, which may include a remedial visit, a repair, a replacement or a refund. Where the cause is your work or the conduct of Partner Personnel, you are responsible for the reasonable cost, including any charge or penalty the Client applies to Fixfy, and Fixfy may deduct it from your payouts after telling you the amount and the reason.

B7.2 If you disagree with a deduction, tell Fixfy within 14 days and Fixfy will review it with you in good faith.

B8. Self-Billing and CIS

B8.1 Fixfy pays you for Fixfy Client Work by self-billing, under the Invoicing and Payment Collection Agreement. You must not issue your own invoices for Fixfy Client Work.

B8.2 Where a Booking of Fixfy Client Work is a construction operation within the Construction Industry Scheme ("CIS"), such as painting and decorating, repairs or building work, and Fixfy is the contractor, Fixfy will verify you with HMRC before paying and make any deduction HMRC requires, at the rate HMRC confirms. The deduction is shown on the self-bill, and Fixfy will give you a CIS payment and deduction statement for each tax month in which a deduction is made.

B9. Photos and Reports

B9.1 Photos, reports and other material that you or Partner Personnel create for Fixfy Client Work belong to Fixfy, as set out in the Terms of Use.
